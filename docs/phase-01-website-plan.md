# Phase 1 — HospitalSoftwere website structure and content

Status: planning deliverables complete; product claims and launch inputs remain subject to confirmation.

Prepared: 14 November 2026. This phase defines the marketing website. It does not implement the diagnostic center application or the later website phases.

## 1. Purpose and audience

Help diagnostic center owners understand what HospitalSoftwere does, what the free core includes, and how to discuss setup. Help receptionists, laboratory staff, reviewing doctors, and accountants understand their part of the workflow.

Primary audience: diagnostic center owners and managers in Bangladesh.

Secondary audience: reception and billing staff, sample collectors, technicians, pathologists/radiologists, and accountants evaluating the system.

Primary conversion: a customer contacts HospitalSoftwere about a demo or setup.

Secondary conversion: a customer explores the workflow, previews, free-core scope, or preparation checklist.

The public website will not collect patient records, run billing operations, provide staff accounts, or act as a patient portal.

## 6. Page-by-page content briefs

### Home

H1: **Your diagnostic center, connected from reception to report.**

Purpose: give a concise overview and send visitors to detailed pages.

Section order:

1. Product descriptor, core promise, free-core statement, Explore software and Request a demo actions.
2. One relevant product screen or clearly marked illustrative preview.
3. Four everyday needs: register patients, bill tests, track samples, release reports.
4. Short reception-to-report journey linking to the full product page.
5. Links to Billing & Accounts and Pathology & Reports, with short benefit-led introductions.
6. Free-core summary with a link to complete inclusions and separate service arrangements.
7. Three onboarding preparation steps and a Getting Started link.
8. Contact action.

Keep detailed feature lists, long FAQs, and extension descriptions on their own pages.

### Diagnostic Center Software

H1: **One workflow for your diagnostic center's daily work.**

Sections: who it is for; patient registration and referrals; orders and billing; sample collection and test status; result entry and independent review; released PDF reports; settlement of dues and daily closing; responsibilities by staff role; links to specialist pages and demo.

Visual: an original workflow diagram with roles and handoffs. Make specimen-free imaging paths clear.

### Pathology & Reports

H1: **From result entry to a reviewed, branded report.**

Sections: tests, panels and parameters; units and age/sex reference ranges; abnormal and critical flags; numeric/dropdown/text entry; draft → verified → released; prevention of self-verification; reasoned amendments and preserved versions; branded PDF printing; narrative imaging and special-test templates with optional attachments.

Visuals: clearly labeled sample CBC, narrative report, and amendment example. Use fictional demonstration data. Explain flags as workflow aids without claiming diagnosis or guaranteed medical accuracy.

Explicit boundary: no PACS, DICOM, or advanced image viewer.

### Billing & Accounts

H1: **Keep invoices, collections, and dues in view.**

Sections: test orders and invoices; authorized discounts with reasons; full and partial payments; cash and manual bKash/card recording; outstanding dues; refunds and traceable corrections; collections by user; daily cash reconciliation and closing.

Visual: a worked fictional example—invoice ৳650, cash payment ৳400, due ৳250, later settlement ৳250. Show sales and collections as separate figures.

Avoid implying bank reconciliation, payroll, expense accounting, full general-ledger accounting, or gateway verification.

### Features

H1: **Explore what the free core is designed to cover.**

Group the feature inventory by Patients & Referrals, Catalog, Billing, Samples, Results, Reports, Daily Operations, and Administration. Include language support, permissions, audit records, and CSV exports.

Use a navigable feature index and concise explanations, with links to detailed pages. Add a staff-role summary. Label availability based on confirmed implementation status.

### Screenshots & Demo

H1: **See the work before you choose the software.**

Organize previews into Reception, Billing, Samples, Result Entry, Verification, PDF Reports, and Daily Closing. Each preview explains the task shown. Support accessible enlargement and captions.

Use real screenshots only when supplied and cleared of patient information. Until then, label original mockups **Illustrative product preview**. Add a video only when an actual recording is available; no fake play buttons or borrowed footage.

Request a walkthrough links to Contact with demo intent. Do not imply a working software sandbox if one has not been provisioned.

### Free Core & Pricing

H1: **Free core software. Clearly defined scope.**

Sections: ৳0 core software license positioning; one center/one active branch; grouped inclusions; what is outside the core; installation/hosting/training/support arrangements discussed separately; planned extensions; setup next step.

No invented service prices, unlimited claims, lifetime guarantee, open-source license claim, free-hosting promise, or paid subscription requirement for the core. Clearly distinguish a software offer from optional services.

### Extensions

H1: **Additional tools when your center needs them.**

Explain free core versus optional extensions. Each item has a purpose, an availability label, and a contact action. No checkout, purchase button, or working marketplace in this phase plan.

Planned paid extensions: SMS/WhatsApp automation, online payment gateway, analyzer integration, multi-branch, patient portal, and referral commission payouts.

Later possibilities: home collection management, corporate/insurance billing, reagent/inventory management, and offline billing/local sync. Availability and commercial terms must be confirmed before publishing.

### Getting Started

H1: **Prepare your center for a smoother setup.**

Sections: discuss requirements; prepare catalog and templates; configure staff and center branding; validate the workflow and plan go-live. Describe these as onboarding steps rather than a guaranteed installation timetable.

Checklist: center details/logo; staff roles; test/panel list and prices; units/ranges; specimen requirements; invoice/report formats; existing dues or migration needs; printer/label requirements; backup and restore arrangements.

Retain the downloadable checklist and tailored priorities from the existing page. Clarify that a download does not submit a request.

### About

H1: **Focused software for diagnostic center teams.**

Explain the supplied goal: offer the core diagnostic software free and keep the everyday workflow focused. Describe the product's scope and a practical approach to onboarding.

Publish verified provider identity and story when supplied. Do not invent a registered company, office address, team, years in business, customer count, certifications, or founder biography. Supplied contact details can be used immediately.

### FAQs
